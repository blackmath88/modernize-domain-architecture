# WS-08 — Product Landscape

## WORKSTREAM ID

`WS-08`

## MISSION

Study what companies monetize when underlying technical knowledge is open, public, standardized, or reproducible.

## WHY THIS MATTERS

Portable knowledge does not imply absent product value; this workstream tests where durable differentiation plausibly remains.

## QUESTIONS

How do GitHub, HashiCorp, Red Hat, Snyk, Sonar, Datadog, JetBrains, cloud migration vendors, and enterprise developer tools monetize integration, execution, trust, certification, updates, UX, workflow, distribution, hosted infrastructure, governance, support, telemetry, evaluation, or operational data?

## INPUTS TO READ

- `AGENTS.md`, `CURRENT_STATE.md`, `RESEARCH_THESIS.md`
- `docs/product-analysis/provider-influence.md`
- `structured/thesis.json`, `structured/claims.json`
- primary company documentation, pricing, architecture, filings, and credible technical analysis

## TASKS

1. Select defensible comparison units rather than forcing whole-company analogies.
2. Map open substrate, proprietary layer, buyer, value mechanism, defensibility, and failure mode.
3. Separate stated positioning from observed monetization.
4. Compare patterns against the Modernize product-value hypothesis.
5. Identify which value layers require proprietary domain knowledge and which do not.

## EXPECTED OUTPUTS

- `research/product-value.md`
- `structured/product-value-patterns.json`
- `agent-output/WS-08-handoff.md`

## BOUNDARIES

- Avoid superficial analogy and generic market summaries.
- Do not equate open source with free operations or weak differentiation.
- Do not claim access to private revenue or product data.

## DEFINITION OF DONE

- Every required company/category is addressed or explicitly excluded with reason.
- Each analogy names both similarity and important disanalogy.
- Product-value patterns are evidence-linked and prioritized.
- JSON parses and distinguishes documented facts from inference.
- Required handoff, status updates, validation, and commit are complete; a PR is open or its access blocker is documented.

## HANDOFF REQUIREMENTS

Use the format in `AGENTS.md`. Name the strongest monetization precedent, weakest analogy, and implication most likely to change the product thesis.
