## KEY FINDINGS

- **Strongest monetization precedent, INFERRED:** Moderne's licensed private-repository execution and fleet workflow over OpenRewrite. Proprietary recipes and substantial semantic implementation prevent a pure "open knowledge plus hosting" interpretation.
- **Weakest analogy, INFERRED:** Datadog-style operational-data advantages applied to episodic modernization. Recurrence, representativeness, reuse rights and transferable lift are unestablished.
- **Most thesis-changing implication, OPINION:** Test a maintained native operator plus an accepted-change workflow, not a new package format. Existing products are competing alternatives; a price list does not establish a market for another layer.
- **Counterevidence preserved:** Snyk research and Moderne recipes may differentiate knowledge; public concepts are not equivalent to open implementations. Several current source/free offerings have restrictive licenses.
